YAPORA APP STORE

Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters.

Effective date: 15 November 2026

This policy explains how YAPORA APP STORE may collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use our Android application marketplace.

1. Who we are

YAPORA is an independent Android application marketplace. For privacy questions or requests, contact support@yapora.app.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on the features you use, information may include:

  • Account information: email address, username, user/account identifier, and authentication-related information.
  • Developer and listing information: app name, description, version, APK download URL, screenshots, storefront details, and information you submit for review.
  • Usage and download information: app listings viewed, downloads or download history, search preferences, and interactions with marketplace features.
  • Payment information: payment identifiers, order references, transaction status, membership details, and information needed to reconcile a payment. Card, UPI, and other sensitive payment credentials are handled by the payment provider; YAPORA does not intend to store your complete payment credentials.
  • Support communications: information you include when contacting support.
  • Device and technical information: app version, device/OS information, diagnostic events, network information, and security-related signals where made available by the services we use.

Only enable optional device permissions when a feature needs them. The exact information collected can depend on your device settings and the features you choose to use.

3. How we use information

  • Create and manage accounts and authenticate users.
  • Publish, display, review, and manage developer app listings.
  • Provide app discovery, download links, and download history.
  • Process payments, verify transactions, and manage memberships.
  • Respond to support requests and important service messages.
  • Protect the marketplace, detect abuse, investigate security incidents, and enforce our Terms.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot, and improve the service.
  • Meet legal, regulatory, and accounting obligations.

4. Services and third-party providers

YAPORA uses or may integrate third-party services to operate its features. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase: may support authentication, database hosting, and related app infrastructure.
  • Razorpay: processes payments and provides transaction confirmation and payment references.
  • ImgBB: may host screenshots or images that developers choose to upload through its service.
  • APK hosting providers: developer-supplied external HTTPS hosts may deliver application files. Their own privacy and security practices apply.

We do not control independent third-party services. Review their policies before using their services. The exact providers and data flows may change as the app is updated.

5. Sharing of information

We may share information with service providers that help operate YAPORA, where needed to provide a feature; with developers or users when information is intentionally made public as part of a listing or interaction; with payment providers to complete and verify transactions; and with authorities or other parties when required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety, and security.

We do not sell personal information as a business model.

6. Public information

Information you submit for a public developer listing, such as an app name, description, screenshots, and developer-provided links, may be visible to other users. Do not include private or sensitive information in public listing fields.

7. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain account and transaction records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and comply with legal obligations. Retention periods vary by data type and purpose. Backups and records held by third-party providers may remain for their own retention periods.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No internet service, database, or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. Protect your password, use a secure device, and contact us if you suspect unauthorized account activity.

9. Your choices and rights

You can keep account details accurate, control optional permissions through Android settings, and contact us to request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, subject to identity verification and applicable legal requirements. Some information may need to be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, or transaction-record purposes.

To make a privacy request, email support@yapora.app from your registered email address and describe your request.

10. Children's privacy

YAPORA is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the matter.

11. International processing

Our service providers may process or store information on servers located in different countries. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be applied in accordance with applicable law.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as our features, providers, or legal obligations change. We will publish the updated policy here and revise the effective date. Please review it periodically.

13. Contact

For privacy questions, complaints, or requests, contact support@yapora.app.
