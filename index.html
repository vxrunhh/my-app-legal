Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 26, 2023

This Privacy Policy describes how Yapora App Store and Yapora Admin (collectively, "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our mobile applications.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide a secure and functional marketplace for both users and developers. The data we collect depends on how you use the app.

A. For General Users (Browsing & Downloading)

Our user marketplace does not require you to create an account to browse or download applications. We do not collect personal identifiers (like your name or email) for general browsing. However, the app may collect:

  • Usage Data: Anonymous download counts and app interactions to display "Trending" apps.
  • Local Data: Your download history and search history are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.

B. For Developers (Authenticated via Google Sign-In)

If you register as a developer, we collect:

  • Google Account Information: Your email address and Google UID (via Firebase Authentication).
  • Developer Profile: Your developer moniker (name) and agreement acceptance status.
  • App Information: App names, descriptions, package names, icons, screenshots, and external direct APK links (e.g., from GitHub Releases).
  • Payment Information: To verify manual UPI membership payments, we collect the 12-digit UTR/Reference number and an optional receipt screenshot. We do not collect or store your bank account details or UPI PIN.

C. For Administrators

Admin access is strictly restricted to an allowlist. We collect your Google Account email and UID to verify your identity and log all administrative actions (Audit Trail) for security purposes.

2. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services that may collect information used to identify you or facilitate the app's functionality:

  • Firebase (Google): Used for authentication and Realtime Database storage.
  • ImgBB: Used by developers to upload and host app icons, screenshots, and payment receipts.
  • OkHttp: Used to securely stream APK downloads from external servers.

3. Data Security & Integrity

We prioritize your security. Yapora App Store does not host APK files on its own servers. Downloads are streamed directly from external hosts provided by developers. To protect you, the app computes a streaming SHA-256 hash of the downloaded file and validates it against the developer's approved checksum. If the checksum mismatches, the download is automatically rejected.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Developers may request account deletion by contacting us at [Your Support Email]. Upon deletion, your developer profile, submitted apps, and notification history will be removed from our Firebase database. Locally stored data (like download history) can be cleared at any time within the app's settings.

5. Children's Privacy

These services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at [Your Support Email].
